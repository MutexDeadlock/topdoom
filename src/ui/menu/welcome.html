Welcome to TopDOOM

An experimental DOOM engine: the original game as a top-down twin-stick shooter, in your browser.

Jump right in

Click Start new game and you are playing. The basic controls:

W A S D
move
mouse
aim and shoot
Q E
rotate the camera
Space
use — doors, switches, lifts
ESC
pause / main menu

The full list is under Settings › Controls.

Your own WADs

TopDOOM ships with the shareware DOOM1.WAD and Freedoom, so there is a full game to play without owning one.

Your own DOOM.WAD, DOOM2.WAD, or any community-made add-on:
Drag and drop it onto the New Game tab, or open the WAD Library there to add your own collection.

Your files stay on your machine - nothing is uploaded anywhere!
